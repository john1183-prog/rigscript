## 3. Acting A/B comparison (Phase 2)

Do this once everything above works. Use any project (narration is optional; the mouth and sway steps need it) and Acting set to **Normal** in the Appearance tab. Paste `docs/samples/v2_showcase.json` into the script editor: 150 seconds: five acting sections (0 to 123 s) and, from 123 s, a kinetic-text section that belongs to section 4; a caption on screen says which section you are watching. **The method for every step:** tap the "Acting: ... (A/B)" chip at the top left of the preview to flip between OFF and your setting, and replay the same stretch each way (a few seconds is enough). Off must look exactly like the app before Phase 2, except that `act` verbs and emotes still play (they are your directing, not acting).

19. **Settle, 0 to 19 s.** Explain, think, wave, point right, stand. **Look for (Normal vs Off):** the forearm and head trail the body by a hair and arrive a little later; a big move leans back for an instant before it starts and settles with a small overshoot instead of stopping dead; a still figure is never perfectly frozen (a tiny drift). **It is wrong if:** a joint overshoots visibly past the pose, a hand wobbles for more than about a second, or a move jerks at the start or end.
20. **Soft expression changes, 19 to 48 s.** The figure stands still while the expression goes wide, angry, happy, squint, worried, normal. **Look for:** the change eases in over a fraction of a second, with a slight squint just before a surprise or an anger, a small "take" (eyes snap wider, then relax) on surprise, and brows that move with the eyes. Off changes the face in one frame.
21. **Eye movement, the same stretch.** In idle moments the eyes drift to a side and back, with the head following a few degrees later and an occasional blink on a bigger look. It is small on purpose (up to 18 percent of the head radius). **Tell me** if it is too small to see on your phone; `GAZE_CAP` is the knob.
22. **Mood breathing, the same stretch.** Angry (25 to 33 s) should breathe faster and a little deeper than normal, squint (38 to 43 s) slower and shallower, worried (43 to 47 s) quick and shallow. The breath is a movement of about a degree and a half, so this is **subtle by design**: look at the chest line and the head together, on a big screen if you can. With narration the talk sway also grows in angry and happy and shrinks in squint and worried. **It is wrong if:** the breath jumps when the expression changes (it must glide), or the figure looks like it is panting in a calm scene.
23. **Walk, 48 to 63 s.** Angry, then a walk of about 10 seconds, then standing again. **Look for:** no breathing or sway changes while it walks (the walk looks the same as at Off), and after it stops (60 s) the angry breathing comes back smoothly over about half a second, with no pop.
24. **Intent verbs, 64 to 101 s.** One verb every three seconds: nod, shake head, emphasize, surprise, think, laugh, sad, look at (right, then the small circle that appears at the top right), look away, point at (a spot, the circle, left). **Look for:** each starts and ends at the pose it began in, a point reaches without the elbow locking, a look turns the eyes and a hint of the head toward the target. At Off they must look the same as at Normal (verbs are not affected by the Acting setting).
25. **Emotes, 103 to 123 s.** Nine marks near the head, one every two seconds: exclaim, question, sweat, sparkle, heart, idea, anger, zzz, dots. **Look for:** each pops in, drifts up a little and fades in about a second, and moves with the head.
26. **Calmer mouth (needs narration).** Play your narration with the showcase's script (or any project with speech) at Off and at Normal. **Look for:** at Normal the mouth opens and closes smoothly, stays closed in pauses and rounds a little on loud vowels; at Off it flips between a few fixed shapes. Then **export once on Canvas and once on GLES at Normal**: both files must look the same as each other and as the preview.

If you only have ten minutes: steps 19, 22 and 23 with the chip, then the export in 26.

## 7. Tuning guide (Phase 1 part)

- **Force a renderer:** export panel, Renderer: Auto, GLES or Canvas. Auto follows the stored verdict; Re-run GLES check refreshes it. A new installed build or an OS update makes the stored verdict stop applying, and the check runs again by itself.
- **GLES acceptance thresholds** are constants in `app/src/main/java/com/example/engine/GlesAcceptance.kt`: `MAX_OFFSET_PX` 0.75, `MAX_MEAN_ABS_DIFF` 6, `MIN_LAPLACIAN_RATIO` 0.90, `MIN_EDGE_AA_RATIO` 0.85, `MAX_DURATION_DELTA_SEC` 0.1, `MIN_PRESENT_AMPLITUDE` 0.01, `MAX_ABSENT_AMPLITUDE` 0.003, `MIN_SFX_CUE_RATIO` 4. The self-test report prints every measured number next to the limit it was judged against: if a number fails by a hair and the two PNGs look identical, raise that limit a little and say why in `V2_DECISIONS.md`; if a number passes but the PNGs look different, tighten it. Change the constant, push, wait for CI, install the new APK, tap Re-run GLES check. The limits are tested in `GlesAcceptanceTest`, which names the old values: update the test in the same commit.
- **If a tone reads "absent" while audible** (or the reverse), look at `DiagnosticsMedia.decodeAudio` (float PCM, channel count) before touching the audio thresholds.
- **Debug GLES switch:** `GlesFrameRenderer.POLYGON_ANTIALIAS` turns the polygon edge fringe off if thin shapes look too heavy.

### Tuning guide, Phase 2 part (acting)

Almost everything is in one file: `app/src/main/java/com/example/engine/acting/ActingParams.kt`. The three columns `SUBTLE_LEVEL`, `NORMAL_LEVEL` and `BOLD_LEVEL` hold the per-level amounts (change a column to make that setting subtler or bolder); the `const val` and `DoubleArray` constants below it are shared by all levels. Verbs and emotes have their own two places (last two rows). Rebuild after any change; with a change to a constant the matching Python prototype (`tools/acting_proto/`) and the tests pinned to it need the same edit (see the last bullet).

| You want | Change | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Less or more lag between body parts | `leadFollowDelaySec` (per level) | smaller = tighter, larger = floatier |
| Moves that take a little longer to finish | `durationStretchPerLevel` | smaller = snappier |
| Less or more wind-back before a big move | `anticipationFraction`, `anticipationCapDeg`, `anticipationLeadSec`; the threshold `ANTICIPATION_MIN_MOVE_DEG` | smaller = fewer, subtler wind-ups |
| Less or more overshoot and settle | `overshootFraction`, `overshootCapDeg`; bounce stiffness `OVERSHOOT_OMEGA`, damping `OVERSHOOT_ZETA` | smaller fraction or cap = calmer; a higher zeta = fewer wobbles |
| A figure that never freezes (or freezes) | `holdDriftDeg` (the brief's ceiling is 0.3) | 0 = no drift |
| Faster or slower expression changes | `expressionBlendSec` | smaller = quicker |
| Stronger or weaker squint-before and take | `faceAnticipationSec`, `faceTakeScale`; per expression `FACE_ANT_DEPTH`, `FACE_TAKE_AMP` | 0 = none |
| Different brows | `FACE_BROW_HEIGHT`, `FACE_BROW_LIFT` (per expression) | |
| Bigger or smaller eye drift | `gazeScale` (0 turns it off), the cap `GAZE_CAP` (0.18 head radii), how often `GAZE_SLOT_SEC`, how much the head follows `HEAD_TILT_MAX_DEG`, blink on a look `GAZE_P_BLINK` | |
| A calmer or livelier mouth | `mouthOpenScale` (per level), `MOUTH_TAU_ATTACK` and `MOUTH_TAU_RELEASE` (seconds; larger = lazier), `MOUTH_HMAX` (the widest opening), `MOUTH_ROUND_MAX` | |
| Mood breathing and sway | `MOOD_RATE`, `MOOD_DEPTH`, `MOOD_SWAY` (a multiplier per expression: NORMAL, WIDE, SQUINT, WORRIED, ANGRY, HAPPY; 1 = the legacy idle), `moodScale` per level (0.5 / 1.0 / 1.4: how far the table counts), `MOOD_BLEND_SEC` (how long a mood takes to settle), `MOOD_LOCO_RAMP_SEC` (fade around a walk). **Set `moodScale` to 0 in all three levels to remove the mood idles and keep everything else.** | moving a table value toward 1 makes that mood less visible |
| Directed looks and pointing | `VerbParams` in `acting/ActVerbs.kt`: `LOOK_CAP`, `HEAD_LOOK_DEG`, `LOOK_IN`, `LOOK_OUT`, `LOOK_MAX_HOLD`, `POINT_LOOK_SHARE`, and the length of each verb `DURATION` | |
| How emotes look | `SPECS` in `EmoteExpander.kt` (shapes, sizes in head radii, colours) | |

- **One master dial** for all of Phase 2: the Acting selector (Off, Subtle, Normal, Bold). Prefer retuning a level's column to editing a shared constant, so Off stays exactly the old behaviour.
- **Prototype twins.** Each acting model has a Python prototype with the same constants (`acting_proto.py`, `face_proto.py`, `mouth_proto.py`, `verbs_proto.py`, `mood_proto.py`). The unit tests pin the Kotlin to rows the prototype prints (`--emit-reference`), so after changing a constant, change it in the prototype too, run it, and paste the new rows into the matching test (`ActingMotionTest`, `FaceMotionTest`, `MouthMotionTest`, `ActVerbTest`, `MoodMotionTest`). The prototype also redraws its plots, which is the quickest way to see a change before building.
